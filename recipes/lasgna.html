How to Make Lasagna

We are going to make a classic homemade Italian lasagna, a delicious comfort food made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta cheese, and melted mozzarella. This recipe combines seasoned ground beef with a flavorful tomato sauce that is slowly simmered to bring out its rich flavors. Each layer is carefully assembled with tender lasagna noodles, creamy cheese, and savory meat sauce. The lasagna is then baked in the oven until the cheese becomes golden and bubbly. After a short resting period, you will have a hearty, restaurant-quality lasagna that is perfect for family dinners or meal preparation.

Ingredients Required

  • 1 pound Ground Beef
  • 12 Lasagna Noodles
  • 24 ounces Marinara Sauce
  • 15 ounces Ricotta Cheese
  • 2 cups Shredded Mozzarella Cheese
  • 1/2 cup Grated Parmesan Cheese
  • 1 Egg
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 1 small Onion, diced
  • Salt and Black Pepper
  • 1 teaspoon Italian Seasoning
  • 2 tablespoons Olive Oil

Steps

  1. Prepare the Meat Sauce
    • Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
    • Add the diced onion and minced garlic, cooking until softened and fragrant.
    • Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it apart with a spatula.
    • Drain excess grease, then stir in the marinara sauce, salt, black pepper, and Italian seasoning.
    • Allow the sauce to simmer for 10 to 15 minutes.
  2. Prepare the Noodles and Cheese
    • Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
    • Drain the noodles and set them aside.
    • In a mixing bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
    • Mix until smooth and evenly combined.
  3. Assemble the Lasagna
    • Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
    • Spread a thin layer of meat sauce across the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    • Place a layer of lasagna noodles over the sauce.
    • Spread a layer of ricotta mixture over the noodles, followed by meat sauce and shredded mozzarella.
    • Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used, finishing with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese on top.
  4. Bake and Serve
    • Cover the baking dish with aluminum foil and bake for 25 minutes.
    • Remove the foil and bake for another 15 to 20 minutes until the cheese is melted and golden brown.
    • Remove the lasagna from the oven and allow it to rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing.
    • Serve warm and enjoy your homemade lasagna with garlic bread or a fresh salad.
