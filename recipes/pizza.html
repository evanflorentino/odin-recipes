How to make a Pepperoni Pizza

Making a delicious homemade pepperoni pizza starts with preparing a high-quality crust and layering your ingredients carefully. First, stretch or roll out your pizza dough on a lightly floured surface or pizza peel until it reaches your desired thickness. Spread a thin, even layer of pizza sauce over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges for the crust to rise and crisp up. Next, sprinkle a generous amount of shredded low-moisture mozzarella cheese over the sauce, ensuring full coverage so the cheese melts evenly. Arrange pepperoni slices across the top of the cheese, placing them slightly apart as they will shrink and cup as they bake. Transfer the pizza into an oven preheated to its highest temperature, ideally around 500°F (260°C), preferably onto a preheated pizza stone or steel. Bake for about 10 to 12 minutes until the crust is deeply golden-brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned in spots. Let it cool for a minute before slicing and serving.

Ingredients required

  • Pizza Dough
  • Flour or Cornmeal
  • Tomato Sauce
  • Seasoning & Oil
  • Mozarella
  • Pepperoni

Steps

  1. Prep the Oven & Dough
    • Preheat your oven: Set your oven to its highest temperature, usually 500°F (260°C) or 550°F (285°C). If using a pizza stone or steel, place it inside now.
    • Warm the dough: Let your refrigerated dough sit at room temperature for about 30 minutes so it stretches easily.
  2. Shape the Crust
    • Prep the surface: Dust your counter and pizza pan (or pizza peel) with a little flour or cornmeal to prevent sticking.
    • Stretch the dough: Use your hands to gently press and stretch the dough out into a 12-inch circle. Avoid using a rolling pin if you want a fluffier crust.
  3. Layer the Toppings
    • Sauce: Spread about 1/2 cup of pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a 1/2-inch border around the edge for the crust.
    • Cheese: Sprinkle 1 to 1.5 cups of shredded mozzarella evenly over the sauce.
    • Pepperoni: Lay your pepperoni slices over the cheese in a single layer.
  4. Bake & Serve
    • Bake: Place the pizza in the oven and bake for 8 to 12 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and browned in spots.
    • • Cool and slice: Let the pizza cool for 2 to 3 minutes before slicing so the cheese sets. Top with optional red pepper flakes, Parmesan, or fresh basil.
